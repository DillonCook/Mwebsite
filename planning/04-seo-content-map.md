## Cross-Site AEO Rules

### Answer formatting
+ Lead with a direct answer in the first paragraph under the H1.
+ Use question-based subheads where natural.
+ Keep FAQ answers concise and specific.
+ Avoid vague marketing filler.

### Entity consistency
+ Use the same business name format sitewide.
+ Use the same phone number everywhere.
+ Keep the service area phrasing consistent: Wesley Chapel, Florida.

### Topical boundaries
+ Residential only on the public site unless the owner says otherwise.
+ No Tampa Bay or multi-city targeting language.
+ No broad county lists.
