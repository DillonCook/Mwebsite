### Repair page FAQ answers
#### `Why is my AC running but not cooling?`
`Common causes include airflow restriction, electrical issues, frozen components, thermostat problems, or parts that are no longer operating correctly. The right answer starts with diagnosis, not guesswork.`

#### `Should I turn the system off if it is frozen?`
`Yes. If the system is icing up, turn cooling off so the equipment can thaw. Continuing to run it can make diagnosis harder and may increase the chance of additional damage.`

#### `Do you repair older systems?`
`Yes, as long as the repair is still practical. Older equipment is evaluated honestly rather than being written off automatically.`

#### `When does repair stop making sense?`
`Usually when the repair cost is too high compared with the condition, age, and likely reliability of the system. That decision should be explained clearly, not forced.`
